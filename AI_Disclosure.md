# AI Disclosure

**Team PANT, VIT Chennai · Samsung XR Hackathon 2026, Track 1**

| Part of the project | Who made it |
| --- | --- |
| 3D modelling, look and art direction | **Humans.** The face is built on the open MakeHuman / MPFB human model (CC0, made by the MakeHuman community). Every decision about the eyes, eyelids, skin, caruncle, lighting and clothing was directed, reviewed and approved by our team. |
| Python code | **Written with AI assistance.** We used AI coding assistants (Claude and ChatGPT) to help write and debug the Python scripts. We defined the approach and the requirements, reviewed the code, and ran every render and test ourselves. |
| Renders, metadata, video and slides | **Produced by our own scripts and tools.** No AI image or video generation was used: every frame is rendered by Blender's Cycles engine from the scene. |

All results reported in the README, the presentation and the video come from the automated
tests in `Source_Code/tests`, which anyone can rerun.
